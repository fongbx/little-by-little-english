# Attribution and source credits

## Word Atlas content and media

This app uses material from [Word Atlas](https://github.com/designfuchen/word-atlas):

- English headwords, Chinese meanings, pronunciations, example sentences and Chinese translations, common word pairs, CEFR levels, and topic labels.
- Word Atlas illustrations and audio files included in `assets/`.

**Attribution:** Word Atlas contributors — [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This project has been modified from the source: the data is filtered and reformatted, vocabulary is organized into everyday scenes and additional themes, and the material is presented in a new learning interface. The 140 daily phrases and app-specific navigation/interface are additions for this project.

The source maintainers provide the licensed content to the extent of the rights they can grant. The source notes that its content was generated and automatically checked, not individually proofread, and is provided as-is.
